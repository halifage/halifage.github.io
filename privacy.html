yt-flow privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

yt-flow is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

With your consent, yt-flow uses the YouTube Data API to read your channel's name, upload videos, set their thumbnails and add caption tracks. It does not read, collect or analyse any other YouTube or Google data.

What it stores

The OAuth login token is stored only on the owner's computer. A record of each upload (video id, link, schedule) is kept with the video's project files. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's, and no data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Revoking access

You can revoke yt-flow's access at any time at Google Account permissions. Deleting the local token file also removes it from the computer.
